# Customize a brand

Verify logo rights, colors, typography licenses, imagery, voice, and brand constraints. Update role-based tokens and managed assets, then adjust composition as needed. Preserve accessibility, responsiveness, performance, and component contracts. Do not fabricate identity assets or claims.
