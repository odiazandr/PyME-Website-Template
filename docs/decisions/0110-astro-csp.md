# 0110 — Astro owns CSP generation

## Decision

Enable Astro's stable `security.csp` support. Astro generates page-specific hashes for emitted scripts and styles and places the policy in each document. Repository-controlled `netlify.toml` owns stable HTTP response headers, including framing protection, because CSP delivered through a meta element cannot enforce every response-header directive.

The base policy allows only same-origin scripts, styles, fonts, connections, forms, and media; permits local data images; blocks objects; and restricts the document base. Third-party origins require an integration review and the narrowest explicit policy change. HSTS remains a launch decision after the final domain and subdomain consequences are verified.

## Consequences

Inline resources generated by Astro receive build-time hashes rather than a broad unsafe-inline allowance. Adding external scripts, styles, images, frames, form actions, or APIs may fail closed until its CSP and privacy impact are reviewed. Netlify headers remain locally testable configuration but are not production-verified until a real deployment response is inspected.
