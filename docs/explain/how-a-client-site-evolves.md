# How a client site evolves

Customization proceeds through verified facts, brand identity, information architecture, necessary custom components, and finally reviewed integrations. Foundation releases arrive as explicit migrations, allowing each client to preserve legitimate differences while receiving improvements safely.

Canonical policy lives in `docs/spec/template-versioning.md` and the relevant runbooks.
