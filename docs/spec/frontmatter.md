# Knowledge frontmatter

Active knowledge documents under `docs/` declare `owner`, `authority`, `status`, and `answers`. The owner is the document's repository-relative path. Canonical documents own truth; derived documents explain it; scratch documents are temporary. Active documents are indexed. Superseded documents point to a replacement before archival.
